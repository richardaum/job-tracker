at Slate

Description:

  • Own and evolve the brand management experience, including brand kits, font management, and templates.
  • Design and build UI systems to enforce brand consistency across Slate’s editor.
  • Collaborate with the design team to create polished and intuitive user experiences.

Requirements:

  • 5+ years of frontend engineering experience with a proven track record of shipping products.
  • Strong skills in React and TypeScript.
  • Experience in building design tooling or brand management features is a plus.
  • Ability to build complex, stateful UI features and maintain shared component libraries.

Benefits:

  • Equity options.
  • Unlimited PTO.
  • $200/month wellness stipend.
  • Health insurance stipend.
  • Home office budget.
